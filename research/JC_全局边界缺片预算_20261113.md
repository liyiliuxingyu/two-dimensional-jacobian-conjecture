# 实际有限正规补全：边界缺片预算与简单折叠支

承接 [run140 回执](https://chatgpt.com/library?file_id=libfile_62b915dbbc008191864a3f9be2e84a27&account_id=personal)。这里始终使用同一个原源的整个 Keller 映射与其有限正规补全。第一部分从原源边界图导出缺片预算；第二部分使用 Borisov 已发表的补全结构，说明指数二边界的光滑性与有限支型。各部分的依赖分别列出。权威 v5/PDF v3 与原条件台账不改。

设 \(d\) 为几何次数，\(D_j\) 为实际补全的仿射边界不可约分量，\(e_j\) 为法向次数，\(\ell_x\) 为补全点的局部覆盖次数。原源紧支撑 Euler 数等于 1，得到 \(d-1=\int_D\ell\,d\chi_c\)。边界分量正规化为 \(\mathbb A^1\)，相接关系可按森林消元；使用 Borisov 的边界不交结论后得到
\[
d-1=\sum_j e_j+\sum_{x\in D}(\ell_x-e_{j(x)}),\qquad \ell_x-e_{j(x)}\ge0 .
\]
这里的 \(\ell_x\) 和 \(x\) 均属于实际有限补全，不能据此反构造原源点，也不把未分歧缺片认作惯性移片。即使目标分支在同一点交会，也不能把实际补全中的不同中心合并。

# Weighted Euler budget for the affine Stein boundary

Let \(F:U=\mathbb A^2_{\mathbb C}\to T=\mathbb A^2_{\mathbb C}\) be polynomial Keller of generic degree \(d\). Let \(\pi:X\to T\) be the finite normalization in \(\mathbb C(U)/\mathbb C(T)\). Zariski's Main Theorem identifies \(U\) with an open subset of \(X\); put \(D=(X\setminus U)_{\mathrm{red}}\). This is the actual finite completion of one map \(F\).

**Local weights and Euler integration.** There is no isolated missing point: on a neighborhood of one, the two coordinates of \(U\) would extend by normality in codimension two. They give a morphism to \(U\), whose composition with the open immersion agrees with the identity on a dense open subset and hence everywhere, a contradiction. For \(x\in X\) define \(\ell_x\) as the number of generic sheets of a small isolated finite analytic representative at \(x\). Equivalently, it is the local length in the scheme fiber: \(X\) is a normal surface and hence Cohen–Macaulay, and finite equidimensional \(\pi\) over the smooth surface \(T\) is flat. Thus \(\sum_{x\in X_y}\ell_x=d\); on \(U\) étaleness gives \(\ell_x=1\). For \(N(y)=\#F^{-1}(y)\),
\[
d-N(y)=\sum_{x\in D_y}\ell_x.
\]
The weight is 1 on \(U\) and generically constant on each \(D_j\), with only finitely many exceptions on \(D\). Refine a finite semialgebraic triviality stratification of the finite map \(\pi\) by these weight loci. On each target stratum the weighted sum is constant and Euler integration commutes with the finite pushforward, by additivity and the product rule on each trivial stratum. This applies also to the open source \(U\) as a compatible constructible subset. Since \(\chi_c(T)=\chi_c(U)=1\), it yields
\[
d-1=\int_D\ell_x\,d\chi_c(x).
\]
Write \(e_j\) for the generic transverse index along irreducible \(D_j\), not its residue degree. Generically \(\ell_x=e_j\) there. For every \(x\in D_j\), \(\ell_x\ge e_j\): in a small finite representative of degree \(\ell_x\) around \(x\), choose a nearby generic point of \(D_j\); its \(e_j\) sheets are among those of the representative.

**The incidence forest.** Resolve the rational projective extension of \(F\) by point blowups outside \(U\), obtaining \(f:Y\to\mathbb P^2\) with reduced boundary an SNC tree of smooth rational curves. Its Stein factorization is \(Y\xrightarrow{h}W\to\mathbb P^2\); \(W\) is normal finite over the target, \(h\) is proper birational with connected fibers, and \(W\) restricted over \(T\) is \(X\). On \(U\), \(h\) agrees with the earlier open immersion by equality on the common function field. An \(h\)-fiber over \(U\) therefore has an isolated point in \(U\); connectedness makes it the entire fiber. The restriction of \(h\) over \(U\) is an isomorphism by properness, quasi-finiteness, birationality and normality. In particular an \(h\)-fiber over \(D\) lies entirely in the boundary.

The support of \(f^*L_\infty\) is connected: a disconnected decomposition into two nonzero effective divisors \(A+B=f^*L_\infty\) has \(A\cdot B=0\) and \(A^2,B^2\ge0\) by nefness. Since the square of the pullback is \(d>0\), one of \(A,B\) has positive square. The Hodge index theorem forces the other divisor to be numerically zero, contradicting its positive intersection with an ample divisor. Thus the support is a connected subtree. The strict transform \(C_j\) of \(D_j\) is a boundary component outside that subtree. It meets the subtree at most once by acyclicity and at least once because a nonconstant morphism from projective \(C_j\simeq\mathbb P^1\) cannot have image contained in the affine target. Hence \(C_j\cap f^{-1}(T)\simeq\mathbb A^1\). Its restriction to \(D_j\) is proper, birational and quasi-finite, hence finite and the normalization of \(D_j\).

For \(x\in D\), a zero-dimensional \(h\)-fiber has one point by connectedness. Otherwise its one-dimensional components are contracted boundary curves and form a connected subtree. Any point outside their union would be an isolated component of the fiber, again contrary to connectedness. The subtree can attach to a given \(C_j\) by at most one edge: two attachments would create a cycle. Thus there is exactly one normalization point over each \(x\in D_j\). At a singleton fiber a point on two distinct \(C_j\) is an SNC intersection upstairs; subdivide that edge at a vertex for \(x\). At a positive-dimensional fiber contract its connected subtree to a vertex for \(x\). These subtrees for distinct points are disjoint. Contracting disjoint connected subtrees and subdividing edges preserves acyclicity. Retaining the vertices for the \(D_j\) and points \(x\) on at least two \(D_j\), with edges for their incidences, therefore gives a forest, with connected components corresponding to the connected components of \(D\).

**The nonnegative budget.** Let \(J(x)=\{j:x\in D_j\}\). The finite bijective normalization of \(D_j\) gives \(\chi_c(D_j)=\chi_c(\mathbb A^1)=1\). Pointwise correction to generic weights gives
\[
d-1=\sum_j e_j+\sum_{x\in D}\left(\ell_x-\sum_{j\in J(x)}e_j\right).
\]
This sum has finite support, since the generic degree is constant on a dense open subset of each curve and component intersections are finite. In each connected incidence-tree component \(K\), choose a root divisor \(r_K\) of maximal index. For each multiple point \(x\), denote its unique parent divisor by \(p(x)\); for each other point let \(p(x)\) be the sole component. At a multiple point all other incident divisors are its children. Each nonroot divisor is a child exactly once, so subtracting the child weights cancels precisely the nonroot weights from the first sum. Consequently
\[
d-1=\sum_K\max_{j\subset K}e_j+
       \sum_{x\in D}\bigl(\ell_x-e_{p(x)}\bigr).
\]
Every excess term is a nonnegative integer by the local bound. In particular \(d-1\ge\sum_K\max_{j\subset K}e_j\). A single actual boundary component of generic index 2 has residual budget \(d-3\); this bounds the number of centers with \(\ell_x>2\), without identifying them with singularities of the target curve. At \(d=6\) the residual is 3, which need not consist of three ordinary cusps without further local hypotheses.

**Prior stronger geometric input.** A. Borisov, [On the Stein Factorization of Resolutions of Jacobian Self-Maps of P²](https://people.math.binghamton.edu/borisov/documents/papers/ampleramification.pdf), Corollary 3.1 and Proposition 3.3, implies disjointness of the affine boundary components for a noninvertible Keller map: the projective Stein boundary intersections occur only at the common image of the proper transform of the source line at infinity, which maps into target infinity. With this prior input the formula simplifies to
\[
d-1=\sum_j e_j+\sum_{x\in D}(\ell_x-e_{p(x)}),\qquad d-1\ge\sum_j e_j.
\]
The forest argument above is independent of that disjointness theorem. Neither formula here is a Jacobian Conjecture conclusion.
# Optional addendum: a simple-fold affine boundary is smooth

Keep the actual finite completion \(\pi:X\to\mathbb A^2_{\mathbb C}\) of a hypothetical noninvertible polynomial Keller map \(F:U=\mathbb A^2\to\mathbb A^2\), with affine boundary \(D=X\setminus U\). Use A. Borisov's [On the Stein Factorization of Resolutions of Jacobian Self-Maps of P²](https://people.math.binghamton.edu/borisov/documents/papers/ampleramification.pdf), Proposition 2.11, Corollary 3.1, Proposition 3.3 and Theorem 3.4. The Stein completion is intrinsic to the function-field extension; the relative contractions used to arrange the minimal model in Proposition 2.10 preserve the map on the original \(U\) and its Stein completion. In that model the affine boundary components are pairwise disjoint. Each is smooth in the smooth locus of \(X\). At a possible affine singularity \(x\) on a component \(D_j\), the relevant reduced total boundary on the smooth resolution has the form
\[
C-E_1-\cdots-E_k,
\]
with \(C\) the smooth strict transform of \(D_j\), \(E_i\) the contracted rational curves, and all joins single transverse intersections. No other noncontracted boundary component meets this chain over \(x\).

Contract any exceptional \((-1)\)-curve in this chain, repeating until none remains. Castelnuovo contraction stays within the resolution of \(x\) because the map to \(X\) factors through each contraction. If the curve has two neighbors, their two distinct points on the exceptional curve become two distinct tangent directions after blowing down; the neighbors meet transversely. If it has one neighbor, that neighbor remains smooth. This includes contractions adjacent to \(C\). Thus the new graph is still an SNC chain of the stated form, with a smooth \(C\). If \(x\) is singular, the resulting chain is nonempty, since a proper birational morphism with no exceptional curves over a normal surface point is an isomorphism nearby. Its intersection matrix is negative definite, so the remaining exceptional self-intersections are \(-b_i\) with \(b_i\ge2\). This is the minimal resolution at \(x\).

On this local smooth resolution pull back the nowhere-vanishing target form \(dU_1\wedge dU_2\). Its vanishing divisor is a canonical divisor. Near the fiber its only possible components are \(C,E_1,\ldots,E_k\), because off the actual boundary the original map is étale. The coefficient on \(C\) is \(a_0=e-1\), where \(e\) is the generic transverse ramification index of \(D_j\). The coefficient \(a_i\) on \(E_i\) is at least 1: both target functions are constant along \(E_i\), and their differentials are proportional there at a general smooth point. Adjunction gives \(K\cdot E_i=b_i-2\). Taking \(a_{k+1}=0\), intersect the vanishing divisor with each \(E_i\):
\[
a_{i-1}-b_i a_i+a_{i+1}=b_i-2.
\]
Thus \(c_i=a_i+1\) obeys \(c_{i-1}-b_i c_i+c_{i+1}=0\), \(c_0=e\), \(c_{k+1}=1\), and \(c_i\ge2\) for \(1\le i\le k\). In particular
\[
(c_{i+1}-c_i)-(c_i-c_{i-1})=(b_i-2)c_i\ge0.
\]
The last difference \(c_{k+1}-c_k\le-1\), so each of the \(k+1\) differences is an integer at most \(-1\). Their sum is \(1-e\), proving \(k\le e-2\). In particular \(e=2\) rules out any affine singular point on \(D_j\). The conclusion uses no assertion about a lift of \(D_j\) to the local quotient chart.

There is also a bound on the cyclic quotient order. Put \(P_0=1\), \(P_1=b_1\), and \(P_i=b_iP_{i-1}-P_{i-2}\), the determinant of the leading \(i\times i\) minor of the negative chain intersection matrix. Inductively \(0<P_{k-1}<P_k\); iterating the recurrence for the \(c_i\) gives
\[
e=P_kc_k-P_{k-1}c_{k+1}=P_kc_k-P_{k-1}>P_k.
\]
The local singularity is cyclic by Theorem 3.4. Its order \(n\) is \(P_k\): the lens space link has first homology of order \(n\), and the chain plumbing presents this group by the intersection matrix. Equivalently this is the determinant/numerator formula for the Hirzebruch–Jung continued fraction of the minimal cyclic quotient resolution. Therefore \(n\le e-1\). If \(e=3\) and the affine point is singular, the only possibility is a single \((-2)\)-curve and order \(2\).

At every point on an index-two component both \(X\) and \(D_j\) are now smooth, and locally \(\det(d\pi)\) is a unit times one reduced equation of \(D_j\). Rank zero would make all entries of the Jacobian vanish at the point and hence make its determinant vanish to order at least two. Rank two is excluded by vanishing of the determinant on \(D_j\); therefore rank is one. Choose analytic coordinates giving \(\pi=(u,H(u,v))\), centered at the point, and subtract \(H(u,0)\) by target shear. If the local degree is \(m\), then \(H(0,v)=b v^m+O(v^{m+1})\) with \(b\ne0\), since the fiber ideal is \((u,H)\); also \(m\ge2\). For \(m=2\), the reduced boundary \(H_v=0\) solves for \(v\) as a function of \(u\), and its image is a smooth branch with unramified normalization map. For \(m>2\), \(H_{vv}(0,0)=0\); smooth reduced vanishing of \(H_v\) forces \(a=H_{uv}(0,0)\ne0\). Thus \(H_v=0\) solves for \(u=A(v)\), where
\[
A(v)=-mb\,v^{m-1}/a+O(v^m),\qquad
H(A(v),v)=(1-m)b\,v^m+O(v^{m+1}).
\]
This parametrizes a single target branch with orders \((m-1,m)\). Their gcd is 1, so the map from the smooth local boundary to the normalization of that branch has local degree 1. In particular \(m=3\) produces an ordinary cusp, \(m\ge4\) produces a branch of multiplicity \(m-1\), and \(m=2\) produces a smooth branch.

In fact the residue degree of such a component is 1. Its normalization is \(\mathbb A^1\) by the global forest argument, and it is itself smooth. Its finite map onto the normalization of its irreducible image is unramified at every finite point by the preceding local calculation. Extend the map to the smooth projective completions; the target has genus zero by Riemann–Hurwitz from \(\mathbb P^1\), and each omitted target point must be hit by the only omitted source point. The target normalization is therefore \(\mathbb A^1\), and the finite unramified map \(\mathbb A^1\to\mathbb A^1\) has degree 1. This argument concerns the residue degree of the actual component, and gives no assumption about the number of other components over the same target curve.

For an actual single affine boundary component of index two in degree six, the local excess is \(m-2\) and the sum is 3. Without a further restriction on branch singularities, the allowed partitions remain \(3,\ 2+1,\ 1+1+1\), i.e. one \((4,5)\) branch, one \((3,4)\) branch together with one ordinary cusp, or three ordinary cusps. The branches counted here may share a target point. This classification neither asserts existence of any listed case nor removes the global hypotheses for higher degree bounds.

## 对六片三尖点接口的一个归约

再加 \(d=6\)、完整非适当曲线 \(C\) 不可约、至少一条实际边界法向指数为 2，并且 \(C\) 至少有一个非光滑的有限解析支。则每条实际边界都有限支配 \(C\)；其正规化覆盖满射，所以该解析支在每个边界正规化中都有原像。

若有指数一的边界，则上述链估计排除其上补全奇点，且该分量处处光滑，\(\pi\) 在其附近 Jacobian 为单位。它的局部像支均光滑，与指定非光滑支矛盾。若另有指数至少三的边界，泛型支出至少 \(2+3=5\)，而指数二分量在该非光滑支上至少另花一个单位，超过 \(d-1=5\)。若另有指数二边界，则两个分量的泛型支出至少四，且指定支各花至少一个单位，仍超过五。故实际边界仅一条，且前节已证明切向次数为一。若所有有限解析支只许光滑或普通尖点，则总超额 \(5-2=3\) 恰给三个普通尖点支。这里只数支，允许多个支落在同一个目标点。若不限制支型，仍保留前述三种超额分拆；没有排除一般六片映射。
